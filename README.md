# fire-detection
Identify the presence of fire within a structure or area. Once detection occurs, the system activates visual alerts to warn occupants and initiate emergency procedures aimed at minimizing the dangers associated with fire or smoke.

Fire detection applied to
1. Forest Fire Detection
2. Smoking Detection in Public Areas
3. Kitchen Fire Prevention in Residential Building

Challenges and Limitation
1. Variability in Smoke and Fire Appearance
2. Limited Training Data
3. Limited Environmental Adaptability
4. Dependency on Optimal Lighting Conditions:
